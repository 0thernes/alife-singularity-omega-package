# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our scientific research community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive scientific environment include:
- Rigorous, evidence-based technical criticism focused on claims, proofs, code, and data rather than individuals.
- Transparent disclosure of assumptions, failure modes, null outcomes, and AI-tooling usage.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive academic peer review and red-team scrutiny.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, fabrication of verification results, or publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the maintainer at `0_0@0thernes.art`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
